Services

EAA compliance services, from a rapid read to a full audit

A clear ladder, so you can start small and go as deep as your product needs. Every engagement begins with the same free assessment, and each step is a proportionate next one — not a package you have to buy whole.

Our evaluations follow WCAG-EM 2.0, the W3C methodology for credible accessibility evaluation. That shapes how we work: we scope by journey rather than by page, we test the way disabled people actually use a product, and we never sell a sample as a whole-product conformance claim. A quick automated pass cannot certify compliance, and we will not pretend otherwise.

Free Assessment — €0

A 20-minute orientation call. A read on whether your consumer-facing journeys are likely in scope, where they broadly stand, and what a proportionate next step looks like. It is the place to start, and it always counts toward what comes next.

Rapid Exposure Check — €350

A fast expert read of your most critical journey for the barriers that matter most. In WCAG-EM 2.0 terms this is a preliminary evaluation: it finds the obvious barriers and tells you where you stand. It is not a full audit, and never a compliance certificate. So you know quickly whether you have a real problem, and how big.

Accessibility Statement — Assessment from ~€600, Creation from €950

Assess an existing statement against what the EAA actually requires, or create a compliant one: EN 301 549 named, honest conformance, and a real feedback route. Creation scales with the number of products or services covered. So you have the first document an authority requests, done properly.

Critical Journey Assessment — €1,500–€2,500

Up to three of your most commercially critical journeys tested against EN 301 549, with prioritised findings, a remediation plan, and a documented compliance position. A fixed price because the scope is bounded up front. The right starting point for most organisations. Beyond three journeys or unusually complex flows, a Full Product Audit is the better fit. So you know what compliance requires for your specific product.

Full Product Audit — from €3,800, scoped to your product

The whole product against EN 301 549, and how real users actually experience it — the human method as standard, not an add-on. The price scales with the product: a focused web product typically runs €3,800–€5,500; larger or multi-platform products (more journeys, a mobile app, dashboards) are scoped and quoted at the free assessment. Optional user research with disabled participants (from €7,500, scoped to the research plan, with participant incentives at cost) and mobile testing (from €1,500, scoped by platform). So you see everything between you and your customers, and what it is costing.

Compliance Framework — from €12,000

The governance system around the audit: accountability, monitoring, and the dated evidence trail enforcement bodies actually look for. So you can demonstrate active management to a regulator, board, or procurement team.

Remediation Re-test — from €750, scoped

Once you've implemented fixes, we re-test the journeys flagged in your assessment to confirm the barriers are actually resolved and nothing new was introduced along the way — a re-evaluation in the WCAG-EM 2.0 sense. You get a dated before/after. So you can prove the fixes worked, for your evidence trail or your board. Often folded into the ongoing partnership below.

Ongoing Compliance Partner — from €1,200 per quarter

Someone owning the standard between audits: monitoring of the covered journeys, a recurring checkpoint in your release rhythm, and a maintained evidence trail. So you keep it fixed rather than watching it drift with every release.
